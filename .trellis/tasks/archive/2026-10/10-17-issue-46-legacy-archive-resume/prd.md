# Selected legacy archive resume — Issue #46

## Goal
Restore execution of the same manually archived predecessor Task without inventing formal completion or replacing its identity. Source: https://github.com/castbox/Trellis/issues/46; downstream integration owner: castbox/guru-trellis#577.

## Requirements
1. Independent public maintenance inspects a selected archive and prepares reviewed conversion/resume without its old dispatcher or current binding. Support only existing positively recognized legacy and strict schema1 families at `.trellis/tasks/archive/<month>/<directory>`.
2. Preserve TaskId, source, accepted scope evidence, planning, metadata and historical delivery facts. Keep original archive readable and outside execution authority. Unknown formats, ambiguous ownership and current schema formal archives receive concrete diagnostics.
3. Require explicit caller-owned resume intent and history attribution. Never infer successful gates or completion from status, dates, PRs or Issue state. No empty Delivery, Completion, Closure or Finish. Pending private closeout obligations return to their owner.
4. Restore one active Task at the same generation: no formal completed generation exists to reopen. Preserve formal reopen. Historical real outcomes may be explicitly attributed; current work routes to its actual next consumer without inherited gate passes.
5. Freeze exact source, destination, planning projection and selected session/binding actions using one matching CLI/Core candidate. Protect other Tasks, sessions, worktrees and dirty/untracked content. No whole-repository migration or global installation.
6. Retry, lost output and ordinary partial-write recovery reuse the operation and generated identities. Rollback touches original actions only, refuses later work conflicts and incompatible tools/data combinations.

## Acceptance Criteria
- AC1: Representative isolated manually archived predecessor, with planning/history and no Finish, resumes same TaskId/generation. Target-native identity/continuation and next actual public consumer succeed with real session or legal explicit-task route. Raw archive remains readable and not current authority.
- AC2: Historical delivery/current supplement coexist without inferred passes, empty Delivery or manufactured completion; accepted scope/history stay traceable.
- AC3: Schema2 formal reopen, active conversion, history-only closeout and full Plan2 retain semantics.
- AC4: Missing intent/evidence, unsupported formats, stale source, identity/candidate mismatch and ambiguous ownership fail specifically without unselected writes.
- AC5: Reapply/recover/resume, ordinary partial apply and rollback preserve identity/history/write set; later work and unrelated resources survive.
- AC6: Actual source/build/API identities, packed matching CLI/Core verification, affected template parity and target-native proof are recorded separately from Guru overlay joint acceptance.

## Out of Scope
Real business recovery, cross-repository implementation, release, global CLI/node_modules changes, resource cleanup, arbitrary formats, personnel restoration, concurrency/TOCTOU or unusual crash hardening, full multiplatform release certification.

## Decisions
User explicitly authorized task creation, planning, preferred decisions, implementation, commit/push/PR/merge and Issue delivery without intermediate questions. No unresolved product questions. Guru semantics and private transaction recovery remain with their owner.

Note the shape of the baseline top hits: `turns=1`, `turns=2`, `turns=4`. The
denominator is the *post-compaction remnant*, so a session that was compacted
down to two surviving turns scores 48.5 while a 228-turn session that discusses
the topic throughout scores 0.79. The score is not measuring topical density,
it is measuring how badly the session was truncated. Fixing the corpus is
expected to move these hard, and that movement is a correction rather than a
regression.
